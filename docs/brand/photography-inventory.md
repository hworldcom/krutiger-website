## Excluded during initial review

Posts and reels centered on children are not shortlisted by default. They require explicit guardian consent, a clear editorial reason, and approval of the exact placement before reuse. Personal memorial, birthday, and family posts are also excluded from marketing use unless the account owner specifically selects them for the website.

## Approval checklist

Before an image becomes production-ready, record:

- Original filename and uncompressed source
- Source post when applicable
- Photographer or copyright holder
- Website-publication permission
- Consent for every identifiable participant
- Guardian consent for every identifiable child
- Approved credit line, if required
- Meaningful alt-text intent or a decision that the image is decorative
- Desktop and mobile focal point
- Expiration or campaign restrictions, if any
